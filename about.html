About us

A lab built
between disciplines.

We connect tissue architecture with cancer biology, bringing experimental models, spatial analysis and AI together.

Our lab

Established in April 2024, the mAIques Lab is led by Dr Oscar Maiques, Lecturer and Group Leader in Digital Pathology at Barts Cancer Institute, Queen Mary University of London.

We are based in the Centre for Cancer Biomarkers and Biotherapeutics. Our research investigates how the extracellular matrix and immune microenvironment influence cancer, with an emphasis on melanoma and pancreatic cancer.

Oscar’s background

Oscar trained in Biotechnology and completed his PhD at the University of Lleida with Prof Xavier Matias-Guiu and Prof Rosa Maria Marti. He subsequently undertook postdoctoral research with Prof Victoria Sanz-Moreno across King’s College London, Barts Cancer Institute and the Institute of Cancer Research.

He established the lab through the Barts Precision Medicine Programme, with mentorship from Prof Claude Chelala and Prof Carol Dezateux and support from Barts Charity.

Teaching and academic activities

Teaching and supervision

Oscar teaches spatial biology, AI, personalised medicine and digital pathology, and supervises PhD, MSc and MRes research.

He co-leads Cancer Biology and Research Skills (SSC1b, CANM938) from 2026 and is a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy.

Academic service

Oscar is a member of the British Association for Cancer Research and the European Association for Cancer Research, and the BCI delegate to the Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry Data Strategy and Capability Committee.

He contributes to tissue-bank application review, the BCI Communications Committee and the DERI Fellows Network.

Partnerships and involvement

We collaborate with Panakeia Technologies and contribute to the OPTIMA oncology network.

Patient involvement through the Pancreatic Cancer UK Research Involvement Network helps inform our research.
