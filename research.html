mAIques Lab · Barts Cancer Institute

Cancer in context

How do the physical organisation of tissue and its immune neighbourhoods influence cancer progression?

Our approach

Extracellular matrix and mechanics

We investigate how collagen organisation and tissue mechanics affect cancer-cell behaviour, invasion and plasticity.

Spatial immune biology

We use multiplex tissue imaging and spatial analysis to study relationships between cancer cells, immune cells and the extracellular matrix.

Computational pathology

We develop and apply AI methods to study tissue morphology, molecular features and potential biomarkers from pathology images.

From models to patient tissue

Our work combines 3D collagen and organotypic models with multiplex immunohistochemistry, spatial tissue profiling and computational analysis. We use these complementary approaches to test biological mechanisms and investigate their relevance in patient samples.
